Failure, cancel, pause, crash, retry

every path ends in a saved status
  • Child fails: JobFailed
  • Child crashes or runs out of memory: PhaseDied, FAILED
  • Cancel: kill the child, CANCELLED
  • Pause: the phase finishes, the job goes back in the queue
  • App closed or killed: RUNNING becomes QUEUED at the next start
  • Retry: end of the queue, checkpoints reused
QUEUED→RUNNING→ DONEFAILEDCANCELLED
